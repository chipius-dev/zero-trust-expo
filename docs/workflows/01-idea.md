# Phase 1: Idea (Concept & Motivation)

The **Idea Phase** is the genesis of any new feature, project, or architectural improvement. It articulates the core problem, user value, and underlying motivation before spending resources on research or prototyping.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Identify and document the user problem or business opportunity.
- Define initial value propositions and intended outcomes.
- Determine whether this idea warrants progression to research, prototyping, or a PRD.
- Initialize or link an entry in the repository [Roadmap](file:///ROADMAP.md).

---

## 🤖 Agent Operating Guidelines
When a human developer or stakeholder brings an initial idea:
1. **Listen & Clarify**: Capture the raw intent, target audience, and primary pain points.
2. **Avoid Premature Solutions**: Focus first on *what* problem needs solving and *why*, rather than immediately jumping into code or implementation details.
3. **Anchor in the Roadmap**: Add the concept to the `[Backlog]` section of [`ROADMAP.md`](file:///ROADMAP.md) with an initial issue/epic summary.

---

## 📋 Phase 1 Artifacts & Deliverables
- Entry in [`ROADMAP.md`](file:///ROADMAP.md) under `[Backlog]`.
- Problem summary highlighting user need and high-level scope.
- Decision on whether targeted research (Phase 2) or prototyping (Phase 3) is needed.
